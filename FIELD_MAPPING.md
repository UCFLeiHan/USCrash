# Schema and field-mapping entry points

- Final 77-column names, order and definitions: `code/config/canonical_crash_schema.csv`.
- State/version source-field bindings: `code/config/states/<STATE>.json`.
- Category mappings: `code/config/code_crosswalk_<STATE>.csv` and associated detailed crosswalks.
- Configured annual input/output links: `manifests/configured_input_output_links.csv`.
- Source/version documentation: `docs/` and `Unified_MMUCC6/docs/`.

Historical manual notes are retained only as archival evidence; they do not override the active schema and executable mappings.
