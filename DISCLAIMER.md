# Research use and disclaimer

USCrash is an independently prepared research resource derived from state and local crash-data sources. The planned record-level release consists of standardized crash attributes, rather than copies of the original source files. Harmonization does not remove applicable source conditions or establish permission to redistribute every underlying source.

## Accuracy and interpretation

Materials are provided “as is,” without warranties of accuracy, completeness, currency, or fitness for a particular purpose. Source reporting practices, retained populations, available years, and attribute definitions vary. Processing, mapping, and geocoding may introduce errors. Users should evaluate suitability for their analyses and consult the source documentation.

USCrash is not an official agency product. References to source agencies do not imply their endorsement, approval, or validation of the harmonized data or subsequent analyses.

## Privacy and source conditions

Users must not attempt to identify individuals or use the materials unlawfully. The absence of fields intended to contain direct personal identifiers is not a guarantee of anonymity: detailed event locations, dates, times, and identifiers may permit linkage to other records.

Source-specific licenses, attribution requirements, agreements, privacy obligations, and applicable legal restrictions remain relevant. This disclaimer does not grant rights or override those conditions. It does not replace the publisher's own responsibility to establish an appropriate release scope.

## Current release status

The GitHub repository contains the website, code, documentation, mappings, catalogs, and aggregate outputs. Full record-level data and original raw archives are not included. Public record-level download links will be provided only for the release scope supported by the completed source review.
